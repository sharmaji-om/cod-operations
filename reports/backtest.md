## Leakage and limitations

Order IDs are quarantined before splitting. No identifiers, names, phone, exact street text, date or final_status enter model features. History fields are assumed to be pre-order snapshots supplied by the source; their chronology cannot be verified. 140 test phone occurrences also appeared earlier; unseen-phone test metrics cover 272 rows and are in evaluation.json. Phone numbers are not reliable customer IDs, so this is only a sensitivity check.

City uses train-only smoothed residuals capped at +/-2 percentage points by default; it cannot cause rejection. Pincode rates require minimum samples and shrink toward the training mean; unseen/sparse codes fall back. Logistic training uses leave-one-out pincode rates. No arbitrary feature percentages are called probabilities. Scores are statistical estimates with limited sample support, not certified calibration. Date timestamps in the CSV are treated as local business time; no future outcomes are used at inference.

Run a prospective randomized pilot to measure a, b, acceptance, q, reminder costs and repeat-reject opportunity loss before claiming savings. Dataset sample is not extrapolated to 3,000 monthly orders.
